Short version

Timeout is a self-help app that helps you pause during a betting urge. For the current MVP, your urge log and settings stay on your device. We do not run an account system and we do not sell your data. Optional Timeout Plus is billed by Apple. Optional Face ID / device passcode locks the app on this device only.

Timeout is not medical, clinical, or financial advice, and Get help / 1-800-GAMBLER is a way to reach support resources — not a claim that Timeout itself is a crisis or treatment service.

What we collect

On your device (not sent to us in MVP)

  • Urge sessions (times, Held or slip, optional tags, optional protected amounts)
  • Slip / recovery plans you write
  • Profile preferences from onboarding (e.g. goal, optional weekly spend estimate)
  • App settings (appearance, whether you turned on Face ID lock, soft-paywall “seen” flag)
  • The date you last opened Timeout and your “Remind me to cancel if I stop using Plus” choice — used only on this device to remind you that you can cancel Timeout Plus if you stop using it (stored in iOS UserDefaults; reminders are local notifications)

This data is stored locally with SwiftData on your iPhone or iPad.

Not collected by us in MVP

  • No account email or password
  • No HealthKit data
  • No advertising identifiers from us
  • No analytics or crash SDKs that send data to our servers (unless we add them later and update this policy)
  • No contact-list upload (“Text a friend” uses the system share sheet)

Purchases

Timeout Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store / StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to billing. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or via Cancel or manage subscription in the app.

Face ID / device lock

If you turn on Require Face ID, Timeout uses Apple’s LocalAuthentication so Face ID or your device passcode can unlock the app after you leave it. Biometric data stays on the device under Apple’s control; we do not receive Face ID templates. Lock is for privacy on this device only — not for tracking. Get help remains reachable from the lock screen without unlocking.

How we use information

In MVP we use on-device data only to run Timeout for you (timers, streaks, Insights, settings). We do not sell personal data. We do not use your urge log for advertising.

Sharing

We do not share your on-device urge log with third parties in MVP because it does not leave your device to our servers. System features you choose (Share, Mail, Messages) are controlled by Apple and the apps you pick.

Export and delete

In Settings you can:

  • Export my data — create a JSON file on device and share it wherever you choose
  • Delete all data — wipe local Timeout data and return to onboarding

Deleting local data does not cancel Timeout Plus on your Apple ID. Cancel subscriptions with Apple as described above.

Children’s privacy

Timeout is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your region). Do not use the app if you are under the applicable age.

Helpline / Get help

Links and numbers such as 1-800-GAMBLER are provided so you can reach external support. Timeout does not provide crisis counseling itself and is not a substitute for professional care.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example if we add cloud sync, accounts, or analytics). We will post a new effective date on the hosted page.
