Get help

In Timeout, use Get help for helpline resources (including 1-800-GAMBLER in supported regions). That opens external support — Timeout is not itself a crisis or treatment service.

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Suggest an improvement

In the app: Settings → Help us improve → Suggest an improvement. Pick a category, write a short note, then send via your mail app (or the share sheet). Feedback is optional, free, and does not require an account.

Timeout Plus

To cancel or change a subscription: in the app use Cancel or manage subscription (opens Apple’s sheet), or go to iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting app data does not cancel Plus.
